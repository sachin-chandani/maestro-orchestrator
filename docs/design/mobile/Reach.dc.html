How a self-hosted server reaches your phone
There is no Maestro cloud. Your phone talks to your own server, and reminders leave through the providers you chose, with your keys.
Maestro app
installed from your server’s web page
A private, secure link
HTTPS, with no ports opened on your router
maestro serve
web app, API and the scheduler
Your data
SQLite file or PostgreSQL, with nightly backups
Notifications, texts, calls
arrive even with the app closed
Delivery services
web push, Twilio, Telegram, your email
YOUR PHONE
YOUR RASPBERRY PI, MAC OR $5 VPS
sends each reminder when due
Three ways to get the secure link: Tailscale (private to your devices), Cloudflare Tunnel (a public address, no open ports), or your own domain with Caddy (automatic HTTPS). Installing the app and receiving push both need HTTPS, so a plain http://192.168… address works for the browser but not for notifications.
